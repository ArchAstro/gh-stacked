# Code of Conduct

We want participation in `gh-stacked` to be safe, respectful, and productive.

Be considerate, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and respect
different backgrounds and levels of experience. Harassment, threats,
discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and
other abusive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. They
will consider context, impact, and patterns of behavior when responding.

To report conduct concerns privately, use the repository's private
vulnerability reporting form and identify the report as a code-of-conduct
matter. Maintainers will limit disclosure to the people needed to review and
address the report.
